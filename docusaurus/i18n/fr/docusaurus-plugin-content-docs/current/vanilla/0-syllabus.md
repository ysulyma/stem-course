# Syllabus

Ceci est un cours sur le **JavaScript frontend** dans le cadre de la visualization scientifique. It is aimed at anyone who needs to present graphs, code, data, equations, and so on. It is especially aimed at educators (whether in traditional classrooms or edfluencers), but is equally useful for presentations and even research.

The examples will be taken from undergraduate math and data science courses, since that is my background.
